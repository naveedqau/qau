Full Message from Vice Chancellor

Prof. Dr. Zafar Nawaz Jaspal, Vice Chancellor Quaid-i-Azam University

Quaid-i-Azam University was chartered with the explicit mission to serve as a beacon of postgraduate education and research for Pakistan. Since 1967, our faculties have produced world-leading research in Natural, Social, Biological, and Medical Sciences.

With our QS World Ranking standing at #363, QAU remains committed to broadening our academic partnerships across Europe, Asia, and North America. As we approve our upcoming strategic plan for the establishment of the QAU Teaching Hospital & Medical College, we invite scholars and students worldwide to partake in our rich heritage.
